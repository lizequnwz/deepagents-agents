## Final selection and replay

execute_analysis_python now requires analysis_kind, declared before executing the
assignment's forecast preparation. Once forecast intent is declared it cannot be
undone by a later general step or restart. finish_analysis requires final_outputs
(execution IDs to output names) and final_result_ids (successful assignment output
IDs). Completed analysis needs a final selection; invalid/out-of-assignment output
references return repair feedback. Forecast scores attach automatically after
validation of selected predictions. Partial selected analyses cannot be relabeled
as a completed investigation.

Full execution history remains stored. The coordinator receives compact final
outputs; the report and UI omit superseded diagnostics. Published result references
identify material evidence; supporting parents remain in inspection, report
provenance and download lineage. The portable notebook identifies computational
steps as provenance, and the manifest records authoritative analysis_selections.
Replay includes final producers and necessary dependencies, not unrelated trials.
Co-produced datasets from a necessary exact step remain computational provenance.

API/storage contract is **21** and new bundles use format **4**. Restart both
services with fresh incompatible storage, preserving needed older artifacts
separately. No migration, fallback reader or counter-reset recovery was added.

## Budget behavior

The existing native limiter state continues counting calls. The coordinator has
at most eight presentation model calls and sixteen presentation tool calls, plus
the existing presentation time budget. Source SQL, saved-data SQL, Python and new
assignments cannot start after durable analysis exhaustion. A saved report keeps
its supported findings if only presentation fails; deterministic report retry
still works without source access. A model that exhausts even its bounded repair
allowance can leave a failed, recoverable run; it is not called complete and no
unsupported report is fabricated.

## Remaining limits

The model still chooses and declares the analytical intent and method. Structural
checks cannot certify all statistical assumptions, feature availability, source
completeness, causal interpretation or the meaning of a custom COUNT(*)/expression.
The direct entity-count guard is not a general proof of business grain.

Business-equivalent refresh, material quantitative prose/unit bindings and broader
metric identity through arbitrary saved SQL remain separate work. No claim is made
that these gaps are closed. General project-code authoring, external research,
shared hosting and a dedicated execution sandbox remain outside this increment.
Behavioral ablations and provider/user outcome studies remain pending; no rejected
prompt simplification was adopted.
